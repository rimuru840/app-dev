# Even If This Love Disappears From the World Tonight (2022)

### Overview
To stop his classmates from bullying someone else, **Torū Kamiya** makes a false confession of love to **Maori Hino**. She accepts with three rules, only to reveal she suffers from *anterograde amnesia*, meaning her memories reset every time she sleeps.

---

**Key Highlights:**
* **The Diary:** Maori keeps a detailed diary to remember what happened the day before.
* **The Romance:** A heartbreaking story about making every single day count.
* **Cinematography:** Beautiful, soft-toned visuals typical of Japanese youth dramas.

### Notable Quote
> "Even if I forget you tomorrow, my heart will still remember the warmth of today."
